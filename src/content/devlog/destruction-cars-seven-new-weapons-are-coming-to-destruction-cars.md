We've been building new weapons in our test arena, and seven of them are coming to the game next. None of them is in the game yet: they're still being tuned in the lab, and they'll arrive in the campaign, Free Roam and online at the same time, with the same rules everywhere.

![Seven new weapons are coming to Destruction Cars](/images/devlog/destruction-cars-seven-new-weapons-are-coming-to-destruction-cars-1.png)

Here's what each one does. The clips at the end are in the same order, all recorded in the test arena.

## Railgun

Hold to charge, let go, and one slug goes through every car lined up in front of you. It sets them on fire as it passes. It's slow to charge, so you have to plan the shot.

## Flamethrower

A short-range jet of fire. Drive into a crowd, sweep it across, and every car it touches starts burning. The fire keeps hurting them after you've moved on.

## Zombie sheep

Yes, really. You fire a volley of sheep, they land on the car in front, hang on and chew it apart: doors and panels first.

## Oil slick

Drop it behind you when you're being chased. Cars that drive into it lose grip. Light it with an explosion and it turns into a burning pile-up.

## Flak shotgun

Point-blank and brutal. One blast tears the doors off and sets the car alight. Useless from far away, devastating up close.

## Wrecking-ball flail

A wrecking ball on a chain, towed behind your car. Swerve, and it swings into whoever is beside you.

## Bolt-on armour

Not a weapon but a defence: steel plates bolted onto your car. They take the hits for you and tear off one by one, so you live long enough to hit back.

## What's next

We'll bring them into the game in a batch, and we'll post here when they're in. Tell us in Discord which one you want first.
