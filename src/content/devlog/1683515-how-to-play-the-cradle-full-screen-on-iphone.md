The Cradle runs on phones now. One thing iPhone players keep asking: _how do I get rid of the browser bars?_

Safari on iPhone doesn't let web games go full screen with the usual button. There's a simple trick, though, and it takes about 20 seconds.

**Step 1: Open the game page in Safari** It has to be Safari itself, not the browser built into Instagram, Telegram, Discord and similar apps. If you tapped a link inside one of those, choose "Open in Safari" first.

**Step 2: Tap Share** It's the square with an arrow pointing up. On newer iOS versions it may be inside the **⋯** menu.

**Step 3: Tap "Add to Home Screen"** Make sure **Open as Web App** is switched on if you see it, then tap **Add**.

**Step 4: Launch the game from the new icon** Your Home Screen now has a Cradle icon. Open it, tap **Run game**, and turn your phone sideways. No address bar, no toolbars: just the station, the dark, and whatever is waiting down there.

![Development screenshot](/images/devlog/354a7c275b7c.webp)\

**A few tips**

-   **Your saves:** the Home Screen version keeps its own save, separate from Safari. If you already started in Safari, you'll begin again. Once you've finished the game, you can jump back to any part from the **Chapters** menu.
-   **Silent mode:** the game plays sound even with the silent switch on. You can change this in Settings → Sound.
-   **Headphones:** strongly recommended. The Nanny hunts by sound, and so should you.
-   **Older iPhones:** on iOS 18 and earlier, the icon may still open with Safari's bars. Updating iOS fixes it.
-   **iPad and Android:** you don't need any of this. Just tap the full-screen button under the game.

If it doesn't work on your phone, tell me in the comments which iPhone and iOS version you have, and I'll look into it.
