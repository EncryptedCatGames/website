A big polish update is live. Thank you to everyone who played, streamed and sent feedback - most of what's below came straight from you.

## New: a Comfort tab

Settings now has a **Comfort** tab. Everything in it is **off by default**, so the game plays exactly as before unless you turn something on:

-   **Streamer-safe audio** - tames the loudest moments so your stream doesn't clip. Quiet sounds stay as they are.
-   **Reduce flashing** - keeps flickering lights under three flashes a second and removes full-screen flashes.
-   **Gentler jump scares** - quieter screams, less camera shake, faces a little further away. No scene is removed.
-   **Content notes** - a short, spoiler-free list of what the game contains.

## The station, relit

The radio station under the sanatorium got a full lighting pass:

-   Before you restore the power, the station now runs on **dim red emergency lamps** over the doors. Find your way by them - and by your torch.
-   Some places are meant to be explored by torchlight only. You'll know them when you see them.
-   When the power comes back, it really comes back.
-   Light no longer leaks through walls, and every lamp now glows from its own fitting.
-   The ending has a new edge to it. Run.

## Fixes and polish

-   Fixed: lights could stay on (or off) after a Continue or a chapter skip.
-   Fixed: you could slip out through the front door at the very start before it shut.
-   The lift escape now gives you a moment to realise what's on the other side of the door.
-   New or reworked props: the reception desk, washbasins, bedside cabinets, linen, the street door, wall lamps, children's drawings, a few new posters, and more detail in some rooms.
-   Removed a line that read like a puzzle hint but pointed nowhere.
-   Story consistency fixes across notes, documents and a couple of lines - with new voice lines in English and Russian.
-   Lots of small visual fixes: gaps above doorways, puddles, a floating microphone, a clock in the wrong place.
-   Pausing now pauses everything - music, voices, the phone, all of it.
-   Faster first load: the game no longer freezes at 100%, and it prepares the graphics noticeably quicker.
-   Paper notes now look like real paper, and fixed light stripes on desks and tables.
-   The Lullaby ending: the last scene in the transmitter hall is easier to see.
-   Reading is safe. The world now pauses while you read a note or inspect an item - no more scares behind an open page. (Terminals still don't. Watch your back.)
-   Control hints. A short hint shows the flashlight key the first time you look into the dark, and the run key in your first chase. You can turn them off in Settings → Interface.
