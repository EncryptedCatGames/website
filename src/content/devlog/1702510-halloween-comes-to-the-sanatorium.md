Until 1 November, the sanatorium is dressed for Halloween:

-   jack-o'-lanterns are lit through the corridors;
-   a candle burns on the reception counter;
-   the title screen has its own seasonal music and art.

On 2 November it all goes quiet again, and the game is back as it was.

**Also in 0.2.0**

-   **A new opening.** You start in a flash-forward deep under the sanatorium. Then it's "three hours earlier": the front door slams behind you, and the radio call plays while you walk.
-   **A new look.** The colours are colder and muted, and the reds stay red.
-   **Notes and drawings you can read on any screen.** They now scale with the screen. There's a new size setting (Settings → Interface), and you can click a drawing to see it full screen.
-   **Streaming mode** is now a switch in Settings → Interface.
