The Cradle: Episode 01 is on the App Store, starting today. It's the whole game as a proper app for iPhone and iPad.

**[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/the-cradle-episode-01/id6823263497)**

![Hiding in a wardrobe on iPhone](/images/devlog/episode-01-ios-iphone.webp)

The browser version has worked on phones for a while, but Safari always got in the way. iPhone won't let a web page go full screen, the address bar takes up part of the screen, and a stray swipe could take you out of the game. The app fixes all of that. It opens straight into the sanatorium, uses the whole screen, and plays offline.

**What's in the app**

-   **The full game.** The same story, three endings and Chapter Select as the browser and desktop versions, including the new opening and the new look.
-   **Touch controls made for iPhone and iPad.** Left thumb to walk, right thumb to look, with on-screen buttons for hiding and holding your breath.
-   **Controllers.** Connect an Xbox, PlayStation or MFi controller and play with it, menus included.
-   **Game Center.** All 17 achievements, some of them hidden, are on Game Center too.
-   **English and Russian**, fully voiced, with subtitles.
-   **Comfort settings**, the Noir and Retro filters, and everything else from Settings.
-   **No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.** You pay once ($1.99 in the US) and that's it.

It needs iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

![The station's cameras on iPad](/images/devlog/episode-01-ios-ipad.webp)

Before release I played the whole game through on my own iPhone and iPad, start to finish, more than once. It's still the best way to play it: headphones on, lights off, the screen close to your face.

The browser version on itch.io and the Windows, Linux and Steam Deck builds aren't going anywhere. Your progress in the app is separate from the browser's.

If you play it, a rating or a short review on the App Store helps a lot. For a small game from one developer, it's what decides whether anyone else ever finds it.
