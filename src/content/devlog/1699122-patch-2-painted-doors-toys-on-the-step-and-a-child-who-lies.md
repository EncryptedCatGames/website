🎃 **Patch 2 is out**, and the doors have more to say.

**Every level teaches something new**

The clues on the doors now arrive one level at a time, so the game starts simple and keeps surprising you:

-   **Level 1:** just the lantern and the decoration over the door. Easy.
-   **Level 2:** sweets on the step.
-   **Level 3:** 🎨 **painted doors.** Blue, brown or white, and the colours change every game.
-   **Level 4:** 🧸 **toys on the threshold**: a rubber duck, a pioneer drum, a roly-poly doll.
-   **Level 5:** everything at once, and the bulbs are failing.

Each level's card tells you what's new.

**The children talk now**

From level 3, some children whisper what they've heard about Nanny when they hand you a sweet. Listen carefully, because they don't repeat themselves.

And from level 4, **one child lies.** Their hint sounds just like the others. If something feels off, step into their room and have a look around… 👀

**A fresh coat of paint**

The sanatorium's 20 children's rooms are no longer all the same. They have wallpaper, tiles, parquet and linoleum, wall carpets, wired radios, tear-off calendars, a piano, a pram and a sled. The corridor got a worn runner and a notice board, and every landmark in the labyrinth now has its own look, so you'll know the washroom from the boiler room even mid-chase.

**Smaller fixes**

-   Clues at the far end of the corridor no longer fade into black.
-   The children's drawings are bigger, so they're easier to read.

Your saves, records and achievements carry over. Tell us how far you got, and whether you caught the liar, on **[Discord](https://discord.gg/ufYtrP3NRk)**.
